# NNNN. Decision as a short noun phrase

Optional. Store it where the repository keeps decisions and number it in sequence. When a later decision replaces it, mark it superseded instead of deleting it.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Proposed, accepted, deprecated or superseded by NNNN |
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |
| Deciders | Names or roles |

## Context

The problem, the forces at play and the constraints, written so that a reader who was not there understands why a decision was needed.

## Decision

The choice, and the reasons that decided it.

## Consequences

What gets better, what gets worse or costs more, and the follow-up work this creates.

## Revisit when

The evidence, metric or date that would reopen this decision.
