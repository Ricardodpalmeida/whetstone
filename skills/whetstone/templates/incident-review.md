# Incident review: short title

Optional. Write one when the impact or the team's practice calls for it. Keep it blameless: describe systems, signals and decisions, not people at fault.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date and duration | YYYY-MM-DD, start and end in UTC |
| Severity | Level and why |
| Status | Draft, reviewed or actions complete |

## Summary

What happened, who was affected and how it was resolved.

## Impact

Users affected, duration, failed requests, data lost or exposed, and error budget consumed.

## Timeline (UTC)

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| HH:MM | Trigger, detection, key decisions, mitigation and resolution, one per row |

## Contributing causes

The conditions that together allowed the incident, each with its evidence. There is rarely a single root cause.

## Detection and response

How it was found, the time to detect and to mitigate, and what helped or slowed the response.

| Action | Type (prevent, detect, mitigate, process) | Owner | Due | Issue |
